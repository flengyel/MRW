# Sums of odd integers

For each positive integer $n$, the sum of the first $n$ positive odd integers is
$$
1+3+\cdots+(2n-1)=n^2.
$$

Proof. The identity holds for $n=1$. Suppose that it holds for a positive integer
$k$. Adding the next odd integer gives
$$
k^2+(2k+1)=(k+1)^2.
$$
Thus the identity holds for $k+1$, and induction proves it for every positive
integer $n$.
